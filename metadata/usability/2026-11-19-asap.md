# ASAP inventory additions and local review

Checked 19 November 2026. Added three distinct datasets after checking the existing 164 cards for matching titles, URLs and products: indicators, automated warnings and crop calendars. Catalogue now contains 167 entries, including 34 dataset cards. The historical 30-record and 31-dataset audits retain their original scopes. No metadata submitted to CDH and no owner contacted.

## Provider findings

The [ASAP download page](https://agricultural-production-hotspots.ec.europa.eu/download.php), [documentation](https://agricultural-production-hotspots.ec.europa.eu/documentation.php), individual files and distribution-specific JRC catalogue records were checked. Anonymous access was tested with actual observations, not inferred from a public landing page.

- **Indicators:** dated discovery returned 12 products for 21 October 2026, not a complete archive manifest. Rainfall combines CHIRPS v3 Preliminary with AgERA5 Final. Its tiled GeoTIFF supports byte-range reads and has no overviews; this does not establish that the mixed indicator archive is cloud-optimised. Full bytes and physical file count remain unknown. The general JRC reuse notice does not resolve every upstream component's redistribution terms.
- **Warnings:** [provider record](https://data.jrc.ec.europa.eu/dataset/jrc-10112-10001) identifies the dataset DOI and European Commission reuse notice. Both level-1 and level-2 historical ZIP directories, embedded dictionaries and CSV prefixes were read anonymously. Rows include 1 November 2026. Sorted WFS queries on `warning_crop`, `warning_gaul1_crop` and `warning_gaul2_crop` returned 1 September 2026; freshness remains an explicit gap. Warning codes are categorical, not severity ranks. Monthly expert hotspots are a separate product and were not added as part of this card.
- **Crop calendars:** [provider record](https://data.jrc.ec.europa.eu/dataset/jrc-10112-10003) confirms FAO-derived planting, growth and harvest windows matched to satellite phenology. Both complete ZIPs were decoded: 287 national rows and 3,068 subnational rows, covering 75 and 74 country identifiers respectively. These are selected crop–location combinations and typical seasonal windows, not observations for a particular year. Correct joins use `asap0_id` and `asap1_id`; download-page labels contain errors. Useful for interpreting whether a warning falls within a crop's season; missing rows do not imply no cultivation.

The warning and calendar table distributions permit reuse with source acknowledgement under the European Commission reuse notice. The catalogue uses the exact local identifier `LicenseRef-EC-Reuse-2011-833-EU`, preserving third-party exclusions. This does not license separately downloaded boundary geometry or all upstream indicator inputs. Technical-report CC BY terms are not substituted for data terms.

## Independent entry-only tests

Three fresh readers received isolated YAML files and the repository's entry-only test instructions. They could follow only links from their entry and linked provider documentation, with anonymous read-only access. They could not inspect the catalogue, gap register or author research. Exact entry hashes and observations are retained in `2026-11-19-asap.json`.

All three readers decoded and interpreted actual observations. Three reviews complete, zero pending; all three retain metadata gaps.

| Warnings | Kajiado, Kenya, reference date **2026-11-01**: crop and rangeland code **5**, named **Warning level 2 / Warning group 2** | The row describes zNDVIc anomaly over at least 25% of active area; current algorithm lineage and exact interval endpoints remain unconfirmed |
| Crop calendars | Talas, Kyrgyzstan, spring wheat: planting dekads **8–12** and harvest **28–31** | Typical windows, not year-specific dates; selected matched crop–location coverage |

The warning reader decoded 4,364 complete prefix rows using bounded range requests; the second archive was inspected for its file inventory only by that reader. The calendar reader decoded both complete tables and verified both entry checksums. The indicator reader downloaded and decoded one complete rainfall TIFF and separately verified HTTP 206 range support. A warning note-only follow-up made the interval uncertainty explicit; the reader confirmed the final YAML hash without repeating the successful data requests. All recorded final hashes match committed candidates.

The calendar reader also followed the linked method report: its 2013–2016 smoothed-NDVI methodology baseline is historical method evidence, not proof of the current downloads' reference period.

Additional author checks verified the warning WFS/archive date mismatch and a generated Kenya rainfall CSV from the public statistics form. The latter yielded actual rows and is not counted as an independent entry-only result. Full warning CSV histories were not enumerated or downloaded in these checks; history start is provider-reported. No claims of complete spatial or temporal validation are made.

## Local checks and retained gaps

Catalogue schema, duplicate ID/URL and licence consistency checks passed for 167 entries. Exact EC reuse identifier accept/reject checks passed; restrictive or lookalike suffixes were rejected. Astro production build passed. No external metadata validation was requested.

All three YAMLs remain `draft_with_gaps` after local CDH v0.5.0 profile checks. Warnings and calendars lack confirmed dataset versions. Indicators additionally lack a dedicated citation/DOI, reconciled licence and complete licensor identification. Local profile errors reflect these logged fields; controlled-domain vocabulary was corrected before entry-only testing. Inventory review completion is separate from formal profile compliance and direct federation eligibility.

Eleven questions were added to the gap register, all nonblocking for this inventory stage. Provider conflicts are explicit in entries rather than filled with inferred values:

- Indicators: immutable snapshot/version; per-component redistribution terms; full dated byte/file manifest; preferred dataset citation; conflicting SPI/WSI/soil-moisture metadata; upstream licensor identities.
- Warnings: algorithm lineage across HTTP version 7.0, manual v8.0, explorer v9 and remaining zNDVIc text; current route and WFS refresh policy; reference-date interval convention.
- Calendars: file version and underlying reference period; maintenance and coverage limitations. The catalogue reports updates as irregular but does not establish when the current files were last revised.

These questions are for optional owner review through the published JRC ASAP contact. No access request or email was sent.
