# Updated dataset metadata and entry-only tests

17 November 2026. All 25 partial dataset templates were enriched from the provider audit and checked locally. Sources, release-specific access routes, formats, redistribution conditions, download sizes, element counts and unknowns are included in the YAML. Review completion allows gaps.

Three independent readers each received an isolated batch of candidate YAMLs and the test instructions. They followed only entry URLs and linked provider documentation, made fresh anonymous read-only requests and decoded bounded samples. Each result records the SHA-256 of the final YAML tested. Licensing and historical-coverage caveats found during testing were added to the affected entries and reread.

## Results

- 22 entries yielded observations through their intended data or service routes. HarvestStat used its checksum-matched statistics mirror. Two of these interpretations remain partial.
- JECAM yielded a documented subset of the original release. MERGE yielded an unchanged IDMC observation through the documented successor. Complete original archives remain unavailable.
- BURGER’s provider approval route was verified. The test stopped before submitting a request.
- Interpretation is verified for 22 of the 24 observations. Digital Earth Africa Waterbodies has conflicting historical coverage dates. SSP crop projections retain reversed interval-bound labels.
- ForTy now passes the local v0.5.0 profile and is filed under records. The other 24 remain drafts with explicit profile gaps. Across all 31 dataset cards, seven pass the local profile and 24 have gaps.

The reconciliation closes 7 old questions, retains 39 and logs 16 additional questions, leaving 55 open fields across these 25 entries. Unknown licensing identities account for 23 of the remaining fields.

## Corrections discovered during review

- Livestock archives contain 488 density rasters and 488 uncertainty rasters. The earlier 2,800 count included macOS sidecar members. The two-archive download total remains 17,567,143,251 bytes.
- GROW-Africa’s GADM-derived companion boundaries need clarification of redistribution terms. The deposit’s CC BY 4.0 declaration is scoped accordingly.
- CCAFS CMIP5’s legacy download-page restrictions conflict with the later WDCC archive grant. The S3 distribution’s licence is recorded as unknown, and both sources are linked.
- Digital Earth Africa Waterbodies distinguishes a 5% wet outline from a 10% wet core. Its historical end year remains inconsistent across the provider specification.
- CHIRPS v2 daily stays excluded from the shortlist. The WFP entry includes v3 final daily SAT/RNL and dekadal routes.
