# Dataset federation and hosting review

17 November 2026 · All 31 dataset cards in the merged Climate Assets inventory. The [evidence table](2026-11-17-datasets.csv) records each provider route, release scope, licence conditions, range read or access barrier, exact byte count where available and questions for owners. Validation ran locally.

## Can federate now

- **WRI forest-loss drivers v1.3** — primary driver COG; four ancillary tables are separate. CC BY 4.0 allows sharing and adaptation. Credit the creators, link the licence and identify changes. [Provider](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19485190)
- **ForTy 2020 v1.0 confidence tiles** — native confidence COGs; the classification companion is in a separate deposit. The provider lists CC BY 4.0 for the map, including the direct confidence tiles. Cite the versioned Figshare deposit. Earth Engine still shows a placeholder for the requested credit line. [Provider](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/projects_nature-trace_assets_forest_typology_forest_typology_2020_v1_0_collection)
- **UKCEH GMID 1980–2024** — CEDA NetCDF distribution; selective reading tested on monthly reference SPEI. UK Open Government Licence v3 permits copying, adaptation and redistribution. Acknowledge the source: “Contains data supplied by UK Centre for Ecology & Hydrology.” [Provider](https://doi.org/10.5285/f5ce92b0-03e8-4719-82ff-c62e6ebe927b)

Two additional distributions meet the criteria inside mixed catalogues. Digital Earth Africa CHIRPS v2 daily and WFP CHIRPS Dekad v3 each returned an anonymous COG pixel. Their parent catalogues span other formats and terms. [Digital Earth Africa CHIRPS](https://registry.opendata.aws/deafrica-chirps/) · [WFP CHIRPS Dekad](https://api.earthobservation.vam.wfp.org/stac/collections/chirps_dekad)

## What needs work

- **13 releases can be converted and re-hosted using documented permissions and anonymous source data.** Annual global livestock, CROPGRIDS, GROW-Africa, CHELSA v1.2, global agricultural workforce, synthetic socioeconomic population, global critical infrastructure, gridded GDP per capita, SSP agricultural management, Kenya helmet-camera labels, CCAFS CMIP5, GMIA-NEXT and household consumption PPP. Follow each distribution’s attribution terms.
- **Five more need conversion plus access or rights work.** HarvestStat’s anonymous CSV matches the CC0 deposit, while other mirror files need review. Sample Earth requires a guestbook and limits reuse to non-commercial purposes. CRA5’s pinned data licence needs clarification. MERGE v1 is closed and requires clearance for the EM-DAT fields. JECAM’s complete historical deposit is closed, though it lists CC BY 4.0.
- **One needs unpacking and re-hosting.** Quasi-global downscaled CMIP6 totals 20.96 TB as stored on Box. Its country tables are already Parquet inside XZ and ZIP wrappers. The gridded NetCDFs need a layout check after extraction.
- **Four already have a cloud-readable layout but other barriers remain.** Copernicus Dry Matter Productivity requires login. GDPCIR requires a short-lived token available without login and has model-specific terms. Forest Data Partnership’s original tiles require a billing project. FAO drained organic soils is anonymous but carries non-commercial and share-alike terms.
- **Four need a component decision.** Digital Earth Africa, WFP and WorldPop have product-specific delivery or terms. UNFCCC’s public browser supplies its own expiring token and some tables carry third-party terms. **BURGER** additionally requires approval, limits redistribution to non-commercial share-alike uses and has an unverified file layout.
